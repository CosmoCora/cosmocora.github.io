# Jobs

## PostDoc: scouting for Humboldt research fellowship & support for other applications

I am a [Henriette Herz Scout](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/henriette-herz-scouting-programme) for the Humboldt Foundation. 
If you are interested in being hosted in my group in Bielefeld for a postdoctoral Humboldt Research Fellowship and belong to an underrepresented group (in particular including women), please get in touch.

I am also open to act as host for applicants to the [Humboldt Research Fellowship](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship) and other fellowships including the [Walter Benjamin programme of the German Research Foundation](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-opportunities/programmes/individual/walter-benjamin). 

## PhD: host support for independent scholarships

I am open to act as host for applicants wanting to pursue a PhD in my group in Bielefeld with Doctoral Scholarships funded by 
- [German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?status=&origin=4&subjectGrps=&daad=1&intention=2&q=&page=1&detail=57135739). 
- [Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes](https://www.studienstiftung.de/en/doctoral-scholarships/doctoral-scholarships)
- Foundations affiliated with the major political parties (Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung, Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung, Friedrich-Naumann-Stiftung, Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung, Hanns-Seidel-Stiftung, Rosa-Luxemburg-Stiftung), the major Christian denominations (Cusanuswerk, Evangelisches Studienwerk Villigst), the trade union movement (Hans-Böckler-Stiftung), German business (Stiftung der deutschen Wirtschaft), and religious minority communities (Avicenna-Studienwerk, Ernst Ludwig Ehrlich Studienwerk).

Please check the application requirements and deadlines and get in touch well in time before the deadline such that we can discuss alignment of your proposal.
